## 2. Crisis & Inter-Session Contact Protocol (Excerpt)

```text
PROTOCOL PENTRU SITUATII DE CRIZA SI CONTACT INTER-SEDINTE

1. Canale permise de comunicare:
Comunicarea inter-sedinte este strict rezervata aspectelor logistice: confirmari,
reprogramari sau anulari urgente.
Terapia nu se desfasoara prin mesaje text sau aplicatii de mesagerie instantanee.

2. Situatii de urgenta psihiatrica sau risc iminent:
Cabinetul individual nu functioneaza ca linie permanenta de interventie in criza.
In caz de criza acuta sau risc vital:
- Apelati Serviciul Unic de Urgenta: 112
- Prezentati-va la camera de garda a celui mai apropiat spital de psihiatrie
- Contactati Linia Verde Antisuicid (TelVerde: 0800 801 200)

3. Discutarea incidentelor:
Orice situatie acuta intamplata intre sedinte va fi adusa si integrata prioritar
in urmatoarea intalnire stabilita.
```

## 3. Session Transition SOP (Standard Operating Procedure)

1. Pre-Session Grounding (5 minutes prior): review client progress trajectory, key themes from previous note, and establish therapeutic container.
2. Clinical Time Window (50 minutes): structured intervention maintaining firm temporal boundaries.
3. Post-Session Documentation (5 minutes immediately following): structured note capture, fee reconciliation, and next slot confirmation.
