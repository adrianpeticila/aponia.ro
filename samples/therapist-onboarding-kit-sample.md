## 2. Sample Intake Protocol & Informed Consent Checklist

### Pre-Consultation Clearance
- Full client legal identity and emergency contact verification.
- Informed consent for psychological services under Romanian Law 213/2004.
- Explicit agreement on cancellation notice windows (minimum 24 hours).
- Clear declaration of practice scope, limitations, and crisis intervention protocols.

### Data Protection & Clinical Confidentiality (GDPR Compliance)
- Data Controller: Independent Psychological Practice (Cabinet Individual de Psihologie).
- Purpose: Direct delivery of psychological assessment and psychotherapy sessions.
- Legal Basis: Art. 6(1)(b) contractual necessity and Art. 9(2)(h) health provision.
- Retention Period: Strictly compliant with national archiving guidelines for psychological records.
- Third-Party Isolation: Zero advertising trackers, zero algorithmic ad profiling, zero cross-service data leakage.

ACORD PRIVIND SERVICIILE DE PSIHOTERAPIE SI CONFIDENTIALITATEA

1. Cadrul de lucru:
Sedintele au o durata standard de 50 de minute si se desfasoara la intervalul agreat reciproc.
Punctualitatea este esentiala pentru pastrarea ritmului terapeutic.

2. Onorariu si anulare:
Onorariul agreat este achitat prin transfer bancar sau link securizat de plata.
Anularile anuntate cu mai putin de 24 de ore inainte de sedinta sunt facturate integral,
cu exceptia urgentelor medicale dovedite.

3. Confidentialitate:
Toate informatiile impartasite in cadrul sedintelor raman strict confidentiale, conform
Codului Deontologic al Colegiului Psihologilor din Romania.
Exceptiile legale privesc situatii iminente de vatamare sau obligatii judiciare exprese.
