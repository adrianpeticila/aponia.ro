# Patient Intake and Onboarding Flow | APONIA.ro

Clinical triage and patient onboarding in psychotherapy should never feel like a bureaucratic form or an exhausting series of WhatsApp exchanges. APONIA.ro delivers a flow designed for respect, clinical holding, and therapeutic safety.

Why the first contact matters

The earliest moments when a client asks for psychological help shape the therapeutic alliance. When greeted by a cold form or forced to wait hours for a chat response, anxiety increases, and pre-session drop-off rates often exceed 30%.

The APONIA.ro intake protocol solves three vital challenges:

1. A calming space for the patient: Gently phrased questions, unhurried pacing, biophilic design, and seamless completion without requiring a user account.
2. Structured summary for the clinician: Automated synthesis of presenting complaints, relevant history, and availability, delivered directly into the secure client file.
3. Automated crisis detection: Recognition of psychiatric emergencies and immediate guidance toward specialized crisis helplines.

## Stages of the intake flow

### Step 1: Secure invitation
The clinician shares a personalized intake link following the initial booking request or embeds it into their website. The client opens the link on mobile or desktop within an SSL-encrypted, Article 9 GDPR-compliant session.

### Step 2: Initial assessment and empathetic intake
The questionnaire guides the client across core therapeutic checkpoints:
- What brings them to therapy at this moment
- Previous therapeutic history and expectations
- Predominant symptoms (sleep disturbance, anxiety, mood changes)
- Scheduling preferences (in-person office or online video)

### Step 3: Safety filter
If the client indicates suicidal ideation, severe dissociation, or medical emergency, the system immediately presents national crisis phone numbers and notifies the therapist regarding the case sensitivity.

### Step 4: Clinician summary generation
Before the client walks through the practice door or joins the video session, the therapist has a clean anamnesis brief ready on screen:
- Verified contact details
- Three-paragraph clinical summary
- Digitally signed informed consent complying with legal standards

## Compliance and legal protection

All responses are hosted in European Union data centers, isolated with practice-specific encryption keys. No commercial third parties or advertisers ever have access to your clients' sensitive health information.
