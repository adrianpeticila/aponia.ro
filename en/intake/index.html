Clinical Protocol

Patient intake and onboarding without bureaucratic anxiety

The first step toward therapy is often the hardest. APONIA.ro replaces cold forms and fragmented WhatsApp chats with a calming, secure, and clinically structured space.

"Therapy begins the moment the reception space listens attentively, rather than asking the client to fill out a compliance dossier."
Step 1

Personalized invitation without account barriers

The clinician generates a unique intake link or integrates it into their own profile page. The client is not forced to register accounts or set passwords: they access the encrypted form directly on any mobile device or laptop.

Step 2

Empathetic guided intake

The questionnaire unfolds at an unhurried pace, with welcoming language and questions tailored to the therapeutic frame:

  • Primary reason for seeking support at this time
  • Previous therapy experiences and expectations
  • Predominant symptoms (panic, sleep disruption, burnout)
  • Schedule availability and session preferences (in-person or video)
Step 3

Automated crisis and safety filter

If a client mentions acute risk factors or psychiatric emergency, the system responds immediately and discreetly: displaying national crisis contact details (112, crisis helplines) and flagging the case with priority on the clinician dashboard.

Step 4

Ready-made clinician summary sheet

Before the first session begins, the therapist receives a clear synthesis: key issues, needs profile, and informed consent acknowledged under legal standards. Zero minutes lost sorting raw notes or manual copy-pasting.
