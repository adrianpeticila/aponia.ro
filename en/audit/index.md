1. Weekly session volume (under 15, 15-25, over 25).

## What stops the leak in APONIA.ro

1. **Automated scheduling.** Your patient gets one link, picks a free slot, and books without texting.
2. **System confirmations.** Automatic reminders go out before each session, not typed by you at 10 PM.
3. **Clear cancellation rule.** Set it once, and every patient sees it before confirming.
