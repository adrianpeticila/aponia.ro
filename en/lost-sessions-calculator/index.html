frequently asked questions

How does this tool calculate the money lost?

It multiplies three numbers you set yourself: sessions lost per week, your average rate, and your active weeks per year. The formula is in plain sight: sessions × rate × weeks. Nothing leaves your browser.

Where does the 250 RON average rate come from?

From data aggregated on aponia.ro: 142 public individual psychotherapy rates, median 250 RON in June 2026, most between 200 and 350 RON. Adjust the slider to your real rate.

What counts as a lost session?

Any slot that never materializes: the client forgot, cancelled late, gave up after a long message exchange, or the rescheduling died somewhere on WhatsApp. That hour never comes back.

Does the calculator collect any data about me?

No. All the math happens in your browser. No account, no required email, no tracking.

How do I reduce lost sessions?

The most common causes are manual confirmations and WhatsApp rescheduling. A direct booking link with automatic notifications eliminates most of them. That is what APONIA.ro does, at 73 RON per month.
