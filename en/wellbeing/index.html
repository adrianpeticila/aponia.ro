What does biophilic design mean in APONIA?

Biophilic design incorporates natural colors, organic rhythms, and tactile spacing to lower cortisol and engage the parasympathetic nervous system during screen time.

How does Popas help between client sessions?

Popas provides an ambient 8-second breathing loop (4s in, 4s out) that rapidly resets somatic tension, preventing empathy fatigue accumulation.

Are the wellbeing tools available for clients as well?

Yes. In the pre-session waiting room, clients can access gentle breathing guides and grounding anchors to prepare mindfully for therapy.
