A spreadsheet is not a clinical record

When a client decides to seek psychological support, they are in a state of high vulnerability. Sending a Google Forms link conveys a bureaucratic feel: the client feels as if they are filling out an employee satisfaction survey or corporate feedback form.

Furthermore, data submitted via Google Forms lands in a Google Sheet or Google Drive, where it can be accidentally exported, shared via public links, or accessed from synced personal devices. Under European regulations regarding special category data (mental health), collecting sensitive psychological information through unencrypted generic tools constitutes a severe compliance violation.

APONIA.ro transforms onboarding into an act of clinical care. Questions are thoughtfully phrased, the completion pace is gentle, and all answers are immediately synthesized into a clear anamnesis record, accessible on the clinician dashboard in one click.

Frequently asked questions

Why is it risky to use Google Forms for psychotherapy data?
Article 9 of the GDPR classifies data concerning health, including psychological state and psychiatric history, as special category personal data. Google does not provide a compliant DPA for health data on standard accounts, and data stored in unencrypted spreadsheets is easily compromised. APONIA.ro is specifically built to meet strict EU requirements for health data security.
How does the automated client summary help the clinician?
Instead of parsing dozens of spreadsheet columns or reading long, unstructured paragraphs, APONIA.ro compiles a concise summary: primary presenting issue, priority symptoms, treatment background, and any safety alerts. This saves 15 to 20 minutes of raw reading before every new client consultation.
Do clients need a Google account to complete the intake form?
No. Google Forms often forces login when security restrictions or file uploads are enabled. With APONIA.ro, the client clicks the secure link received via SMS or email and completes the questionnaire directly on any phone or computer within an encrypted, SSL-protected session.
What happens if a client indicates an acute crisis or imminent risk?
APONIA.ro includes a discrete clinical safety safeguard. If client answers flag self-harm risk or psychiatric emergencies, the interface immediately provides national emergency numbers and specialized crisis support lines, while highlighting the priority status directly on the therapist intake dashboard.
