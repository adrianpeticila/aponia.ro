# Rhea, how visible are you as a therapist online

Rhea is a free tool from APONIA.ro that estimates your online visibility as a therapist: Google presence, your own website and social proof.

Before anyone books a first session, they look you up. They read your Google profile, your website and how other clients describe working with you. Rhea reads the signals that are already public and gives you a score out of 100, together with one concrete thing to improve first.

## What you get

- An overall visibility score, from 0 to 100
- Three sub-scores: Google presence, your website, social proof
- A single specific first step, not a long list

## How it works

Enter your name, email and, optionally, phone number and website. The score is computed locally in the browser, deterministically from the details you enter. Nothing is sent to a server to calculate the result.

## What this is, and what it isn't

Rhea is a free orientation tool, not a full audit. The score is an estimate based on public signals. It does not replace a professional review of your online presence.

## For patients

Rhea does not find therapists for patients. It is a tool for practitioners who want to know how they come across in online searches.

## About APONIA.ro

Rhea is built by [APONIA.ro](https://aponia.ro), the Romanian scheduling and practice management platform for independent psychotherapists.
