A spreadsheet is not a clinical record

When a client reaches out for psychological help, they are experiencing significant vulnerability. Sending a Google Forms link conveys a transactional, bureaucratic impression: the client feels as though they are filling out a corporate satisfaction survey.

Additionally, data collected through Google Forms resides in Google Sheets or Google Drive, where it can be accidentally shared via open links, downloaded to personal hardware, or synchronized across unmanaged devices. Under European regulations regarding health data, storing psychological information in unencrypted generic systems is a critical compliance failure.

APONIA.ro turns onboarding into clinical care. Questions are framed with therapeutic delicacy, the pacing is unhurried, and information is structured into a clean anamnesis brief accessible on the clinician dashboard in one click.

Frequently asked questions

### Why is it risky to use Google Forms for therapy client intake?

Article 9 of the GDPR classifies health information, including psychological status and psychiatric history, as special category personal data. Standard Google accounts do not offer a compliant healthcare Data Processing Agreement (DPA), and spreadsheets stored on shared cloud drives create significant breach liabilities. APONIA.ro is strictly architected for European medical and mental health data requirements.

### What happens if a client indicates suicidal thoughts or crisis in the form?

Google Forms has no clinical awareness: it simply inserts another row into a spreadsheet that you might not review for days. APONIA.ro features an empathetic safety protocol: if critical crisis flags are triggered, the client is immediately presented with national emergency numbers and support hotlines, while the clinician receives a prominent priority notification on the intake record.

### Do clients need a Google account to complete the intake form?

No. In Google Forms, features like one-response limits or file attachments frequently demand a Google sign-in, causing friction and confusion. With APONIA.ro, clients open a secure link on any smartphone or computer and complete the intake without creating an account or remembering passwords.

### Can I export client records if I need to archive them?

Yes. APONIA.ro allows you to export each client record as a secure PDF or standardized clinical archive, ensuring full compliance with national professional archiving guidelines.
