# Mental Health Data Privacy and GDPR Security | APONIA.ro

Data protection in psychotherapy is not a mere checkbox on a form. It is a direct extension of professional secrecy and the therapeutic trust relationship.

Why mental health data is different

In clinical practice, collected data extends far beyond names and phone numbers. It encompasses emotional history, trauma, family dynamics, psychiatric medication, and intimate vulnerabilities.

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), this information falls under Article 9: Special categories of personal data. Processing is legally prohibited unless grounded in an explicit legal basis and reinforced with technical security safeguards.

Core pillars of the APONIA.ro security architecture

### 1. Exclusive European Union hosting
All databases and backups reside on servers located within the European Union. Your client data never transits or rests in third-country jurisdictions (such as the US), eliminating risks tied to international data transfers.

### 2. Practice-level encryption (Zero-Knowledge)
Session notes, intake evaluations, and anamnesis records are encrypted at the field level. The decryption key belongs solely to the clinician. No APONIA.ro staff member, administrator, or automated background task can inspect your clinical notes.

### 3. Data Processing Agreement (DPA) aligned with Romanian and EU regulations
Every individual psychology practice or professional partnership receives a bilateral Data Processing Agreement tailored to the College of Psychologists guidelines and national healthcare standards.

### 4. Zero ad tracking and zero profiling
Unlike generic scheduling tools or commercial messaging apps, APONIA.ro never includes third-party trackers (no Meta pixels, no behavioral ad scripts). We do not sell data, analyze private conversations, or generate consumer profiles.

### 5. Seamless right to erasure and portability
Both clinician and patient retain complete control. Upon concluding the therapeutic relationship or upon verified request, records can be exported in full into a secure clinical format or permanently deleted, respecting statutory medical archiving terms.

- Your practice is shielded against regulatory penalties and professional compliance audits.
