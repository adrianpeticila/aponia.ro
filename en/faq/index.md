# FAQ | APONIA.ro (EN)

> Canonical: https://aponia.ro/en/faq/ | Last updated: 2026-10-14

## 1. Is APONIA.ro for solo practices or large clinics?
APONIA.ro is built for solo practices and independent therapists: own booking link, automated confirmations, session notes. Not clinic software with reception shifts. See /en/about/ About and /en/audit/ Practice Diagnostic.

## 2. Do I need to install anything?
No. APONIA.ro is cloud, zero-install. You work in the browser; the patient books from a link, no account and no app.

## 3. How much does it cost and do I get a VAT invoice?
Cabinet 73 RON/month, Tools 29 RON/month, with deductible fiscal invoice. No commission per session.

## 4. Where is patient data stored and how is GDPR handled?
Data is hosted in the EU (Frankfurt), encrypted in transit and at rest, with strict role-based access. See /en/confidentialitate/ Confidentiality (GDPR).

## 5. Does it sync two-way with Google Calendar?
Yes, 2-way Google Calendar sync: personal blocks close the public link, and new APONIA.ro bookings appear in Google Calendar. Full comparison on /en/vs-google-calendar/ vs. Google Calendar.

## 6. How do cancellations under 24h work?
Free cancellation until 24h before; under 24h it is marked as a late cancellation under your practice policy. Automatic 24h reminder reduces no-shows.

## 7. Must the patient pay online by card?
Not required. Online payment is optional; you can collect in person or by transfer. APONIA.ro takes no commission.

## 8. How do I stop manual WhatsApp confirmations?
Automatic confirmation at booking plus 24h email reminder; confirmations visible in the dashboard. Compare /en/vs-whatsapp/ vs. WhatsApp.

## 9. Is there a free trial?
Yes, 14 days free, no card. Setup in about 5 minutes, link sent the same day.

## 10. How do I cancel if it is not a fit?
One click in your account, anytime, no minimum term and no cancellation fee, with data export before closing.
